Investment Terminal

How it is built

Explained, not just plotted

Every chart carries a numbered caption plus a note on what it shows, how to read it and why it matters — and offers its own data as CSV.

Cached and parallel

Network access is confined to one cached layer, and fan-out lookups run concurrently, so moving a slider never refetches a financial statement.

Honest about gaps

Missing inputs read as unavailable rather than zero, and the exchange rate applied to every converted figure is stated on the page.
